About

Antares is a Warframe-focused Discord bot designed to help alliance and clan.

Made by -AoD-justTrek and -AoD-choobie.

Bot is currently available on a public Warframe alliance server 404 Squad Not Found and provides tools tailored to the game. It offers many commands, such as displaying information about current weeklies/bounties/fissures - "Trackers". Various commands related to warframe.market website. More advanced commands that can show you intresting information about your inventory using AlecaFrame. This also includes the ability to share/check each others builds, stats etc. or view leaderboards to see who has the most of something, like resource or mission played.

FAQ

Q Can I invite the bot to my server? / Can I install it and use it in any server?

A Not yet, but planned. Antares commands are currently available on a public Warframe alliance server. With some commands also available in the bot's DMs.

Q Is the bot only for Warframe?

A Yes. Antares is built exclusively around Warframe.

Q Does my submitted inventory update automatically?

A Not everything updates automatically.

Automatic updates depend on what's available through the Alecaframe public token. Currently, this includes owned/opened relics and trade history.

If data isn't available through the public token, it cannot be automatically updated. In that case, Antares uses a snapshot of your last submitted lastdata.dat file.

Guide to Inventory

All commands related to your inventory are listed and explained in here.

1. Sending your inventory to the bot for the first time.

You can do that with /lastdata send command.

- How to find your lastData.dat file?

It is located in %localappdata%\AlecaFrame. You can paste this path into the Windows search bar to open the folder. It is your inventory data and is used by commands like /stats, /lb, /build.

- How to find your AlecaFrame public token?

It is required only once on the first send. Once in AlecaFrame: Go to the "Stats" tab. Press "Create public link". Press "Generate token" to copy it. It is your public token used to access AlecaFrame API.

- How to find your Warframe Market profile link?

It is used by some of the commands, not required. Once in Warframe Market: Go to "My Profile". Simply copy the URL link to it.

2. Updating your inventory

Updating your inventory doesn't require Public Token. To update use /lastdata send command and only send lastdata.dat file. You can also simply DM the bot with the file - without any command/text. See "Does my submitted inventory update automatically?" in the FAQ.

3. Changing privacy settings of your inventory

You can do that with /lastdata settings command. By default, after sending your inventory it's public. See it's explanation in here.

4. Removing your inventory from the bot

You can do that anytime with /lastdata remove command.
