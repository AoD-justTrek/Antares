FAQ

Q Can I invite the bot to my server? / Can I install it and use it the bot any server?

A Not yet, but maybe in the future. Antares commands are currently available on a public Antares Community discord server and other selected servers.

Q Is the bot only for Warframe?

A Yes. Antares is built exclusively around Warframe.

Q What does update automatically and what does not?

A It depends on AlecaFrame.

What can and is updated automatically daily: all owned relics, opened relics, trades, platinum, endo, credits, aya, vitus, orokin ducats.

For anything else Antares uses latest snapshot of the submitted lastData.dat file.

Utility

Various handy or helpful commands.

[item]

Item search. Shows basic info about a given item.

For example: [lith g15], [arcane energize].

/relics

Look up relics by part, set, resurgence, or ducats in rewards.

/legend

Send maps for Tent/Mother locations to your DMs.

/price_history

Shows price history for a given item.

/wfm user

Shows warframe.market user info.

/wfm elites

Shows top 100 warframe.market users ranked by reputation.

/wfm highest_price

Shows top highest priced items on warframe.market in a given group.

/wfm highest_volume

Shows top highest volume items on warframe.market in a given group.

Inventory

Commands that work with and can show interesting info about your inventory. All of them can be targeted at another user if their privacy settings allow.

/stats

Shows your stats, has different unique categories.

All categories autocomplete so you can see them all when using the command.

/graphs

Shows a graph of stats history.

/build

Shows a Warframe/Weapon/etc. build.

Important

Commands used to manage your inventory with the bot.

/lastdata send

Command used to send your inventory to the bot, either to register or update it.

/lastdata settings

Command used to view or change your settings related to inventory. To view them, don't use any arguments. To change them, use the setting & toggle arguments.

/lastdata remove

Command used to remove you from the bot completely.

Leaderboards

Commands that show rankings between users that have registered and are in the current server.

/lb item

Shows a leaderboard for a specific item.

/lb category

Shows a leaderboard for a category. All categories autocomplete so you can see them all when using the command.

/lb set

Shows a leaderboard for a specific prime set.

Sniper

A feature that will make the bot send a private message to you if it finds any warframe.market orders matching your snipelist.

/sniper add

Adds an item to your snipelist.

/sniper remove

Removes an item from your snipelist.

/sniper update

Updates an item in your snipelist.

/sniper list

Shows your snipelist.

/sniper mute

Mute or unmute notifications. Either everything, a group of items, or specific item.

Guide to Inventory

All commands related to your inventory are listed and explained here.

1. Sending your inventory to the bot for the first time.

You can do that with /lastdata send command.

Q How to find your lastData.dat file?

It is located in %localappdata%\AlecaFrame. You can paste this path into the Windows search bar to open the folder. It is your inventory data and is used by commands like /stats, /lb, /build.

Q How to find your AlecaFrame public token?

It is required only once on the first send. Once in AlecaFrame: Go to the "Stats" tab. Press "Create public link". Press "Generate token" to copy it. It is your public token used to access AlecaFrame API.

2. Updating your inventory

Updating your inventory doesn't require Public Token. To update use /lastdata send command and only send lastdata.dat file. You can also simply DM the bot with the file - without any command/text.

3. Changing privacy settings of your inventory

You can do that with /lastdata settings command. By default, after sending your inventory it's public. See its explanation here.

4. Removing your inventory from the bot

You can do that anytime with /lastdata remove command.

Guide to In-Game Name

Small guide related to changing your in-game name.

Why there's a guide for it?

When you change your In-Game Name, AlecaFrame treats it as a new "account" and your stats & trade history are lost. Antares prevents that and can preserve it.

  • If you are using Antares, you are basicly already set

After changing your in-game name, simply update your public token.

That means generate a new one in AlecaFrame and update it with the /lastdata send command.
