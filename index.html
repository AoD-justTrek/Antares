About

Antares is a Warframe-focused Discord bot designed to help alliance and clan.

Made by -AoD-justTrek and -AoD-choobie.

Bot is currently available on two discord servers and provides tools tailored to the game. It offers many commands, such as displaying information about current weeklies/bounties/fissures - "Trackers". Some market commands. More advanced commands that can show you intresting information about your inventory using AlecaFrame. This also includes the ability to share/check each others builds, stats etc. or view leaderboards to see who has the most of something, like resource or mission played.

FAQ

Q Can I invite the bot to my server? / Can I install it and use it in any server?

A Not yet, but maybe in the future. Antares commands are currently available on a public Warframe alliance server. With some commands also available in the bot's DMs.

Q Is the bot only for Warframe?

A Yes. Antares is built exclusively around Warframe.

Q Does mine and others inventory update automatically?

A Not everything updates automatically.

What updates automatically daily: all owned relics, opened relics, trades, platinum, endo, credits, aya, orokin ducats.

For anything else Antares uses latest snapshot of the submitted lastdata.dat file.

Guide to Inventory

All commands related to your inventory are listed and explained in here.

1. Sending your inventory to the bot for the first time.

You can do that with /lastdata send command.

Q How to find your lastData.dat file?

It is located in %localappdata%\AlecaFrame. You can paste this path into the Windows search bar to open the folder. It is your inventory data and is used by commands like /stats, /lb, /build.

Q How to find your AlecaFrame public token?

It is required only once on the first send. Once in AlecaFrame: Go to the "Stats" tab. Press "Create public link". Press "Generate token" to copy it. It is your public token used to access AlecaFrame API.

Q How to find your Warframe Market profile link?

It is used by some of the commands, not required. Once in Warframe Market: Go to "My Profile". Simply copy the URL link to it.

2. Updating your inventory

Updating your inventory doesn't require Public Token. To update use /lastdata send command and only send lastdata.dat file. You can also simply DM the bot with the file - without any command/text.

3. Changing privacy settings of your inventory

You can do that with /lastdata settings command. By default, after sending your inventory it's public. See it's explanation in here.

4. Removing your inventory from the bot

You can do that anytime with /lastdata remove command.

Guide to IGN

Small guide to In-Game Name and how it works in Aleca.

Why there's a guide for IGN?

Because when you change your In-Game Name, AlecaFrame treats it as new "account" and resets your stats & trade history. Antares now provides a simple way to preserve it / display it / continue it.

So about changing it

After changing your in-game, and seeing Aleca use that name, simply generate new public token and send it in /lastdata send command, along with required file - basicly the same way registering for the first time is done. The bot will notice and handle it properly.

After that's done your account stats history is preserved & simply continued with different In-Game Name - like it should. You can view your continuous history in a /graphs command that shows all graphs similiar to the ones in Aleca:

Or your trading history continued in /market history - might add more to it later. You can change your name however many times you got platinum for.
